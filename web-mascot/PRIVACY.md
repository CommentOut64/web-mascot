# Privacy Policy for Web Mascot

**Last updated:** October 2026

Web Mascot ("the Extension") is an open-source browser extension designed to bring interactive desktop mascot pets to web pages. We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency.

---

### 1. No Data Collection
**Web Mascot does NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any user data or personal information.**

Specifically:
- **No Personally Identifiable Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or device IDs.
- **No Web History or Page Content:** The extension does not log, monitor, or transmit the websites you visit, page titles, URLs, or any page content.
- **No Keystroke or User Activity Logging:** The extension does not record keystrokes or track your personal browsing activity. Mouse and scroll events are handled strictly in real-time within your local browser runtime to animate pet physics and movement, and are never saved or sent anywhere.
- **No Remote Telemetry or Analytics:** The extension contains no analytics SDKs, trackers, or advertising libraries.

---

### 2. Local Storage Usage
The extension uses the standard browser `chrome.storage.local` API solely to store your own configuration preferences locally on your device, such as:
- Mascot enable/disable toggle
- Selected mascot character (e.g. Neuron, Eviling, or Both)
- Mouse click-through preference
- Scroll interaction preference

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing extension data.
